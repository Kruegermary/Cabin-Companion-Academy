# 🏡 Cabin Companion Roadmap

*"Learning One Log at a Time"*

---

# Mission

Cabin Companion is a cozy digital homeschool cabin where families learn, grow, pray, play, and explore together.

School is only one room in the cabin.

The goal is to make opening Cabin Companion feel like coming home.
